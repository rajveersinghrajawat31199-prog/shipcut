Active-card treatment (THE SIGNATURE MOTION):
- On the left edge of the currently-active card, a 3px-wide Tally vertical
  bar spans the card's full height.

  down to the next card (240ms, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)).
- This is the "playhead" — brand-critical. Do not use CSS animation
  fill-mode "backwards" (causes flicker on state change); animate via
  Framer Motion or a controlled useEffect + inline style transition.

## 5. Global motion rules

- All state transitions use var(--ease) — cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)
- Durations: 120ms (hover/color), 240ms (state changes), 480ms (initial page fade-in only)
- NO transform: scale on buttons or cards
- NO bounce, spring, or wobble
- NO shadow-based hover elevation
